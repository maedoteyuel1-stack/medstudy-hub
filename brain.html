01

What is the Brain?

The brain is the control center of the nervous system. It receives information, processes it and sends instructions to the rest of the body.

🧠 Thinking

The brain helps you think, learn, solve problems and make decisions.

💭 Memory

Your brain stores and retrieves information from your experiences.

🏃 Movement

The brain sends signals that help control voluntary movement.

02

Major Parts of the Brain

Each part of the brain has important jobs.

Frontal Lobe Planning • Decision-making • Personality • Movement
Parietal Lobe Touch • Sensation • Spatial awareness
Temporal Lobe Hearing • Memory • Language
Occipital Lobe Vision • Visual processing
Cerebellum Balance • Coordination • Movement
Brainstem Breathing • Heart rate • Vital functions

03

How is the Brain Protected?

The brain is delicate, so the body has several layers of protection.

🦴

Skull

The skull is a strong bony structure that protects the brain from many injuries.

🛡️

Meninges

Three protective membranes surround the brain and spinal cord.

💧

Cerebrospinal Fluid

This fluid cushions the brain and helps protect nervous-system structures.

04

🧠 Quick Brain Facts

Neurons

The brain contains billions of neurons that communicate using electrical and chemical signals.

Central Nervous System

The brain and spinal cord together form the central nervous system.

Two Hemispheres

The cerebrum has left and right hemispheres that communicate with each other.
