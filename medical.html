🧠 Neuroscience

Your journey into the brain and nervous system starts here.

The Brain

The brain is the main organ of the central nervous system. It controls movement, sensation, thought, memory, emotions and many automatic body functions.

Frontal Lobe Planning • Movement • Personality
Parietal Lobe Sensation • Spatial Awareness
Temporal Lobe Hearing • Memory • Language
Occipital Lobe Vision
Cerebellum Balance • Coordination
Brainstem Breathing • Heart Rate • Vital Functions

03

📖 Medical Terms

Build your medical vocabulary one word at a time.

Neuron

A specialized cell that transmits nerve signals.

Synapse

The junction where communication occurs between neurons.

Dopamine

A neurotransmitter involved in movement, motivation and reward.

Serotonin

A neurotransmitter involved in mood and sleep.

Acetylcholine

Important for muscle activation and nervous-system signaling.

Reflex

A rapid, automatic response to a stimulus.

05

🔬 Neuroglia

Neuroglia, also called glial cells, support and protect neurons.

Astrocytes

Help support neurons and maintain a healthy environment in the brain.

Oligodendrocytes

Produce myelin around neurons in the central nervous system.

Microglia

Help protect the nervous system by acting as immune cells.

06

🩺 Neurological Diseases

Learn basic information about some conditions affecting the nervous system.

Stroke A condition where blood flow to part of the brain is interrupted.
Epilepsy A neurological condition involving recurrent seizures.
Parkinson's Disease A disorder that can affect movement and coordination.
Meningitis Inflammation of the protective membranes around the brain and spinal cord.

07

💊 Medicines

Learn about general categories of medicines used in neurological care.

Anticonvulsants

Medicines commonly used to help control seizures.

Analgesics

Medicines used to help relieve pain.

Antiparkinsonian Medicines

Medicines used to help manage symptoms of Parkinson's disease.

⚠️ Medicines should only be used according to advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
